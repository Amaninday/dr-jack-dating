MEET RABBI DR. JACK COHEN

Clarity before certainty.

Dating and marriage decisions are deeply personal. Dr. Jack's role is to help you slow the process down, understand what is really happening, and make the decision in front of you with greater wisdom and confidence.

Rabbi Dr. Jack Cohen Dating Coach · Marriage Coach · Matchmaker

THE ROLE

Not someone who makes the relationship decision for you. Someone who helps you see it more clearly.

The goal is not to tell you what to choose. It is to help you understand what you are choosing.

When emotions, expectations, attraction, family pressure, fear, and uncertainty become tangled together, it can become difficult to distinguish a genuine relationship concern from anxiety, unrealistic expectations, or a pattern that keeps repeating.

Dr. Jack approaches those conversations with directness, compassion, practical thinking, and respect for the Jewish values and future you are trying to build.

THE APPROACH

See the relationship more clearly.

“My role is not to make your decision for you. It is to help you see yourself, the other person, and the relationship clearly enough to make a wiser decision.”

Good guidance should not create dependency. It should give you greater clarity about yourself, your choices, your relationship patterns, and the qualities that genuinely matter in a lasting marriage.

What happens when we look beneath the obvious?

THE CONVERSATION

Instead of immediately asking whether someone is “right” or “wrong,” we examine what is actually driving the uncertainty.

01

We identify the pattern.

What keeps happening across dates, relationships, attraction, rejection, communication, or commitment?

02

We separate signal from noise.

Which concerns reflect genuine incompatibility—and which may be coming from pressure, perfectionism, fear, assumptions, or unrealistic expectations?

03

We ask better questions.

The goal is not simply to collect more information. It is to understand which information actually matters for the relationship and future you want.

04

We determine the next step.

Continue dating? Have a difficult conversation? Reconsider an assumption? Slow down? Move forward? The next step should come from clarity rather than panic.
