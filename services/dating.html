YOU MAY RECOGNIZE THIS

The confusion may not be about this one person.

When the same uncertainty follows you from one introduction to the next, the most useful question may not be “Is this the one?” It may be: “What am I repeatedly doing, expecting, fearing, or overlooking?”

  • 01 You lose interest as soon as someone becomes available.
  • 02 You stay too long because starting over feels unbearable.
  • 03 You confuse anxiety with a warning—or chemistry with compatibility.
  • 04 Family pressure makes it difficult to hear your own values.
WHAT WE WORK TOWARD

Date from self-knowledge, not panic.

01

Recognize the pattern

Understand the decisions and beliefs that quietly shape who you choose and how you respond.

02

Clarify what matters

Separate values and compatibility from perfectionism, noise, and other people’s expectations.

03

Turn insight into a practical next step for the relationship or date in front of you.
